Research

Independent research · June 2026–present

State-Aware Adaptation and Control of Language Models

When an intervention changes a model, what changes in its parameters, activations, and cached history? I build controlled experiments around LoRA, activation steering, and KV caches to separate these effects.

My current work investigates when cached computation can be reused or corrected under model adaptation, and how to evaluate the quality–compute trade-off.

Undergraduate research project · 2023–2024

Knowledge Graphs for Computer Organization

At Beijing University of Technology, I contributed to a research proposal on building a course-specific knowledge graph. The design combines a domain ontology, educational-text processing, entity recognition, and graph storage.

The documented proposal specifies BiLSTM-CRF and Neo4j; its literature review includes BERT-based entity prediction.

Selected project

PyTorch · Language model implementation · 2026

Decoder-Only Language Model, from the Components Up

A self-directed project based on Stanford CS336 Assignment 1: byte-level BPE, causal attention, RoPE, RMSNorm, SwiGLU, AdamW, and a training pipeline. The portfolio release adds configurable embedding/output weight sharing and top-k sparse-MoE layers.

52 tests pass locally. Additional checks cover routing gradients, causality, and shared-weight serialization. Short synthetic training runs validate all three configurations; they are not language-model quality benchmarks.

The release builds on my learning implementation and includes AI-assisted completion and extensions. This is independent study, not a Stanford affiliation.
